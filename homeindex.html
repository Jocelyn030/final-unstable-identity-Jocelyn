Unstable Identity and the self as constructed Two cursors  ·  One divided self ENTER
ABOUT THIS WORK

Two cursors move through the same space simultaneously — one yours, one its mirror. They share a position in time but never the same intention.

This work asks: if a reflection acts on its own, which one is real? Hover over a word in one mirror and watch its meaning surface in the other — not as an answer, but as an echo.

Inspired by Jorge Luis Borges' The Circular Ruins, in which a man dreams another person into existence, only to discover he himself is someone else's dream. Identity here is not found — it is constructed, reflected, and always slightly out of reach.
